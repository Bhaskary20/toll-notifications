    CO Division KPIs Status Update

    Downloads fresh FMC, BG, notice and ongoing contract data from the portal, then fills the Word template for you.

    What the report updates
    • Cover date
    • Contracts overview, Top 10 agencies and Annexure Table 5 (contracts without CA start / end date and duplicate Toll Plaza IDs are removed first)
    • FMC and BG sections with trend tables and RO-wise summaries

    • Annexure Tables 1, 2 and 6
    • Every other section is kept as it is in the template
